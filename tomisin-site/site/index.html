Featured case study

Cif Editorial Carousel Campaign

A premium social campaign built around Cif green, product storytelling and clean editorial composition.

RoleVisual Designer and Art Director
FormatFive separate 16:9 slides
BrandCif
FocusProduct storytelling and social content

The brief

Turn the supplied Cif references into a premium carousel system that makes everyday cleaning feel aspirational, placing products in a fresh, contemporary world of cleanliness, care and comfort.

Challenge. One cohesive premium campaign from the supplied references.

Objective. Five independent slides that also read as one visual story.

The approach

Colour. Cif green leads, supported by cream, white and soft neutrals.

Type. Large editorial lettering with restrained supporting copy.

Composition. A clear order of image, headline, copy and navigation, with generous negative space.

Intended outcome

  1. Stronger visual consistency across the carousel
  2. Clear use of Cif green for brand recognition
  3. Balance between product visibility and lifestyle storytelling
  4. Premium editorial tone without losing product clarity
  5. Flexible layouts for future Cif campaigns
  6. A polished presentation for a design portfolio
